## Sebastian Lukas
Sebastian is a hard-working individual who tries to prove his worth with every day that he’s on duty. Relatively new to the Oak Vale community, he moved here from the big city of Washington roughly 8 years ago and has been the sheriff for the last 5. Something caused him to all those years ago, most likely some sort of tragedy, but he tends to keep his past close to his chest. But even though he has some secrets, one thing remains crystal clear: Sebastian he cares about this community and pays special attention to the kids here (not creepy). 

Sebastian tries his best to keep the youth of Oak Vale off the street and help them back on their feet. Even though most of the youth here tend to resist the help and are untrusting of his attention, Sebastian always lends an oddly useful and well-timed helping hand. 

Sebastian lives alone in the downtown area of Oak Vale, in an apartment for one. He always presents himself confidently and with a smile when dealing with the public, but those who are nearest to him know the pain and loneliness behind his eyes.
