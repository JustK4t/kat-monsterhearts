Only slightly off the beaten path, the Old Quarry produced limestone aggregate for cement until about 10 years ago. As nature reclaimed the place, more and more routes through the fence were found. 

The two main pits serve as excellent swimming holes in the summer but the steep cliffs and unsupervised nature provide a steady stream of emergency room visits. There are a handful of old buildings and concrete ramps that serve as a skate park for the local teens.

The quarry was recently purchased and re-fenced by Brookhaven National Laboratory for unknown reasons.
