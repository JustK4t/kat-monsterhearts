### Full Cast

#### Title Screen

### Saturday Sep 4, Morning

It was a busy morning for our main characters, for timekeeping purposes we divided the day into Morning/Afternoon/Evening/Night and then had a bunch of simultaneous solo scenes for the morning.

**Aliya** 
We opened with Aliya, apparently vampires can and do get violently hungover. She's in bed head pounding, wearing sunglasses because the early morning hint of a sunrise behind the blackout curtains is still too bright. She planned on sleeping in, so something must've woken her up this early... Oh right, [Aunt Winnie](./_Adults.md#morwenna-raventhorne) is practically beating down her door. Aliya must be in big trouble. Aunt Winnie refuses to go away until Aliya opens the door then immediately turns on the light and makes Aliya sit on the bed. The Raventhorne rule is you can have as many parties as you want at the nursery but any *issues* that arise are your fault and your responsibility she reminds Aliya, treating her like a child. First on the list of grievances is that grandpa Jasper (one of the elders, her fought on the wrong side of the civil war and wwii) was out of the catacombs and almost fed on two girls enjoying each other's company on a bench across the street (The girls were Amy and Trish, but that is not revealed here). Did Aliya not tell people to stay on the property? Does she not understand that random teenagers getting drained inside the gates of Lion's Court creates incredible headaches for the family for decades? Not in the mood for Aliya's excuses, Aunt Winnie continues to the second issue. Someone stole a portrait of Nisha off the wall last night, you know the *only* one that had been made before she left. Aunt Winnie is pissed and misses Nisha dearly and realizes how much this probably hurts Aliya and leaves her with a final task. Unclog the upstairs toilet before you go anywhere.

Aliya texts Zelda who points out she was being an ass at the party and seems annoyed with her. Zelda points out that Flea's Market might be a good place to start before the conversation ends. Aliya attempts to con Elric into fixing the toilet, but she's done that so many times he doesn't even open his door.

Aliya is much more upset about the portrait than the potential double murder and heads to the pawn shop for info. She tries to see if Cleo is inside before going in, but the windows are dirty and there are so many stacks and shelves it's hard to tell. She finally catches a break and Cleo isn't there. Flea is, and lets her know no one has come in since last night and that he can keep an eye out if she would like. Aliya is too self-absorbed to give many details so it gets awkward and Flea points out she might want to go talk to Cleo while she's here since something she said at the party hurt his feelings terribly. Aliya leaves instead.

**Graye**
Next, Graye rolls out of bed and dives into Pokémon Sapphire on their Game Boy Advance (Please don't DMCA this Nintendo). Not that it matters, but we established they are on the 5th gym (Petalburg City, Normal Type) and have a team made of only dog and wolf looking Pokémon. Downstairs, their mom is cooking breakfast and their dad is sitting at the table waiting for them to come down. Graye misses several pointed looks between their parents and probably a few throat clears from dad before he finally just asks them directly to please put the game down so we can talk. 

Graye's dad is clearly uncomfortable with the topic and starts out with "Graye, are you being safe?", which seems like it's leading into a sex talk, but actually is leading into a "There sure have been a lot of mysterious animal attacks, we don't have to worry that it's you right?" talk. Graye denies attacking anyone, swears they are being safe, and we get the banger exchange of "I know what it's like feeling invincible at your age" "yeah? Well were you a fucking werewolf?". Another detail mentioned is that camp daylight is closing because there have been more attacks. The werewolf who turned Graye is for sure still out there, so Graye's parents warn them again to be safe tonight and breakfast is served. Graye's dad tries to relate to Pokémon by talking about his own gym experience and the scene ends not with a bang but with a cringe.

**Amy**

Amy first texts Cleo, who is grumpy and doesn't want company. They then text LC who agrees to meet at Glenn's diner. Instead of the Diner, Amy goes to LC's house, entering the unlocked garage and donning a discarded jacket. She sits on the couch and plays PS2 on the big fancy TV until receiving an angry text from LC "where do u get off :/ did u just set me up for fun". Amy claims stuff at home got real and LC replies "word". Amy leaves their house with the jacket and pockets a set of house keys too.

Amy then has a super normal text exchange with Trish that goes something like:
T: "Hey!"
A: "Hey"
A: "Whats up?"
T: "What are we?"
A: "What?"
T: *10-minute delay in replying*
T: "Nvm something came up, talk later!"

**Everette** 
Trish texts him bright and early, super excited about something. Since he's a boring and slow texter she wants to meet at the diner so they can talk about it, and she can see his face for the big reveal! Everette finishes his level of viewtiful joe and sneaks out without encountering his father. Trish doesn't know the game and assumes Everette is "*handling* ;)" someone named Joe.

Everette doesn't take that long to get to the diner but does eat shit on a bad kickflip on the way there, luckily the bleeding has stopped by the time he does get there. Trish has ordered a stack of pancakes, glass of OJ, and a cup of coffee for each of them. Everette demolishes his and Trish takes one bite of pancakes the whole scene. When Everette finishes his pancakes and drinks he moves on to eating hers.

Trish is super proud of herself for taking the leap and finally getting Aliya to hook up with her, but also disappointed because Aliya did not live up to expectations and was kind of an unexciting hookup, mediocre kisser, and not to mention the humiliation of the whole party finding out. How does Trish know Aliya was a mediocre kisser if that was her first kiss and also hookup? Glad you asked, it's because Amy blew her mind outside on a bench afterward. Which was also super scary but also kind of exciting? Good thing no one saw them because that would be more embarrassing than getting cheered coming out of Aliya's room. Blah blah blah Amy is the clear winner, Aliya is out of her system, and she's super nervous about dating Amy who seems way cooler than her. Everette does a kickflip under the table, smashing the board into one of the empty chairs and knocking it over.

During this exchange Trish has started the text convo with Amy. LC has entered the diner and has ordered a milkshake with two straws.

Trish asks for advice on dating someone really cool and popular since Everette dated Petunia. He explains how the relationship fell apart on its own after she became that way but doesn't want Trish to belittle herself. It is revealed that Trish has more sex experience than Everette now, but they move past it really fast. LC overhears they are talking about Amy and joins the table, sitting on a chair backwards. LC gets the details about Trish and Amy then warns Trish that it seems like she's assuming exclusivity without actually talking to Amy about it. LC leaves, Trish texts Amy "What are we?" then notices Everette is being weird.

They have a couple tries of starting to talk about what's on his mind before Trish manages to ask if it's about Petunia's new boyfriend. This is news to Everette, not surprising but still hurts. Trish is terrible at describing people so all we know is the new boo is taller than Petunia, really cool, and probably has brown hair.

Everette actually really wants to break into the quarry again to find out what's going on with the lab, Trish is reluctant because last time was traumatizing and there was nothing there.

Everette reveals his backstory and undead status!!!!!! By handing Trish the secret book she wants to see so so so bad. Its full of journal entries that should get him inpatient psych help and newspaper clippings, she's fast reader and gets through a big chunk before getting really serious and apologizing he had to go through all that. It looks like she's going to cry when she thanks him for telling her, but she recovers real quick when getting to ask questions about if he wants to eat brains or not. She points out that the journal says he woke up pretty far from the lab and the explosion, and something doesn't add up. She agrees to break into the Quarry again, just not tonight because she needs some sleep. 

**Annalise**
A detail from something Annalise overheard after the sleepover at Chris's place was reiterated for emphasis since it was information that she was meant to have. Chris was in trouble for having girls over *again*, he can't keep doing this.

Hana texts pretty early about getting some coffee at Oh Deer. Lucas is sleeping in and Annalise enjoys a fair amount of laissez-faire, do what you want I trust you, parenting and agrees to go. Hana picks her up in her Mom's car. Gods forbid Kat describes a single visual detail, but the car is a Toyota Camry. The two chat about the party and how it's nice not to have to sneak around since Chris is done being a weenie about it.

When they go in it doesn't sound too busy and Amanda Ram is working. It does have the pervasive odd smell of death that has signals Everette is present. Amanda takes their orders for hot drink and baked good and when the party chatter turns to how Alyssa fainted and that was sort of the end of the party Amanda teasing calls out to Melody about her date later with Alyssa. Hana and Annalise get their orders and go sit with Melody who is just hanging out with Amanda while she works. Melody now smells exactly like Everette when it comes to death, they smell pretty different in terms of deodorant, shampoo and perfume. Everyone is teasing Melody for going on a date with Alyssa despite still being with Balkan, but Melody doesn't see the problem because Balkan has ghosted her for the previous 3.5 days since she nearly died at the quarry. The first hints of open book and full of rage era Melody show as she mentions how mad she is at him and wants to dump him. Talk turns to the accident and melody is once again a totally open book. She agrees to talk about it if they talk about something more fun after!

Talking about the accident sets Annalise on the edge of her seat with worry. Melody is furious, when she was in the water she could hear the people who called the cops talking, debating to call, deciding not to fish her out. She could hear them but couldn't figure out how to move in the water, she doesn't sleep much, and she doesn't remember making it to the shore, but that's where Sebastian found her. Melody is explicitly confirmed to be a ghoul the same way Everette is.

In more fun talking she is super excited about the date with Alyssa! Not put off in the slightest by the fainting thing or the having a boyfriend thing. Annalise is still worried about Alyssa for some reason and suggests a girls night debrief at the diner just to make sure it went well.

### Saturday Sep 4, Afternoon
**Alyssa**

All the morning scenes were interspaced with shots of Alyssa getting ready for the date, she got 'groceries' set the table, tried on most outfits she owns twice, cleaned her room, then dissociated and stared at the wall.

By the time afternoon arrives she is more than ready, and wearing a nice shirt and jeans. Melody texts that she's going to be a bit early! Alyssa waits for her in the parking lot and attempts nonchalance. Amanda drops Melody off and is rolling her eyes at something Melody said as she gets out of the car. She's wearing a strapless blue gingham dress and carrying a wicker basket with a bottle of prosecco in it, she half jogs over to give Alyssa a hug.

Melody is flirting with Alyssa. Hard. It seems nothing Alyssa says can dissuade her. Melody even jokes that she hopes Alyssa isn't a serial killer when she leads her into the woods to the picnic table. Meanwhile, Alyssa is trying to sus out if Melody is into her. She opens the prosecco valiantly and pours it like a pro, deciding Melody is in fact probably flirting with her and wants to be there. 

The talk about how cool it is Alyssa does sword stuff, the party and if Alyssa is ok after fainting, the hand injury. Melody is flirting the whole time. She does bring up Alyssa's parents which isn't a fun topic, but then they go back to fun territory! Melody says this is a good place to start over and meet interesting people, but like any small town you eventually know everyone which is why is fun to get new blood like Alyssa and Amy!  Melody calls Amy a player and supports her burning Streeter, she seems to understand the effect Amy had on Alyssa and thinks its funny.

Alyssa reveals the truth about the deer/horror and Melody believes her, warning of all the folktales of haints and other evils that lure you into the woods never to be seen again. When Alyssa shows her the hand mark she brushes it with her thumb and transitions it into holding her hand.

Melody is once again a scarily open book. She talks about the accident at the quarry and how different everything feels. She's changed, doesn't sleep, and feels unmoored. Melody points out how ridiculous it is to just pretend she's a fast healer when the cuts were that bad a few days ago, but now it's just a scar. She feels empty, likes she's lost something and tries to turn Alyssa on - hoping that the other girl can help her fill that void.

Alyssa is useless and despite a very successful turn on, promises to do everything she can to help Melody out with that. It's only for a second, but there is a flash of rage behind Melody's eyes before she tries again, this time even more directly. She very specifically asks Alyssa to take her back to her room, right now, this is what she wants so badly.

She practically drags Alyssa back to the motel and *metaphorically* jumps her bones the second the door closes. Good thing Alyssa cleaned her room! Before we faded to black Alyssa confesses to not having any experience with another woman, Melody says "Don't worry, I like to teach".

Alyssa's sex move reveals Melody's inner narration of the encounter. She isn't doing as well as the facade she puts on. She's scared of what happened and what she's become. Rage and hunger swirl around a yawning unknowable void, and relief becomes the predominant emotion as the encounter with Alyssa does help to satiate her.

**Amy**
Graye texts to try to make plans for the full moon. They expect to transform with Amy but are also worried Melody is a werewolf and might be dangerous to others on her first full moon. Amy does not respond.

Amy texts Cleo again asking if he wants to talk. He replies that he's by the pool at the Summer Dreams Motor Court (his house). Amy actually goes to this one. The scraping of her chair being dragged over gets her shushed by Cleo who is violently hungover. Amy claims Aliya is possessive among other negative things. Cleo agrees on the possessive but is more interested in finding out if Amy and people in general think he's a liar. The two get flirty when Amy reveals she's keeping her previous promise that "They can do this again" and Cleo implies fae see through her 'like a window'. They go back to his room and we fade to black.

Amy's sex move causes Cleo to enter his darkest self as soon as she leaves and Cleo asks them to promise to tell him "one true thing" from underneath all the layers of bullshit she's built up. She admits to stealing a portrait from the Raventhorne home and Cleo points out that's more of a fact than a truth. She debates for a minute before leaning in and saying, without her usual accent, "At the core of me darling, I'm fucking furious."
