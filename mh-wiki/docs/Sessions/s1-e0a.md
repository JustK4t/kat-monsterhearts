# S1 E0 Pilot - GAE - "Trouble at the Old Quarry"
## Wednesday, Sep 1, 2004
### Graye, Annalise, Everette

### Unknown date, night

We started with a cold open flashback. Watching from a bird's eye view as a massive thunderstorm sweeps through Oak Vale during the night. Sheets of rain batter the roofs, branches whip back and forth until they come crashing to the ground. The streets are empty, and the only illumination is the near constant flashes of lightning. The camera cuts to a girl struggling to collect her belongings into a faded backpack while the river rises around her tent. It's a losing battle. The river is rising too quickly and the storm took her by surprise. She escapes the tent as the water begins to pour in through the seams and stumbles through the water hoping for safety on the higher banks. Her long brown hair is plastered to her head in the rain. The water is up past her knees now and every step is a struggle. We see police lights and the beam of a flashlight approaching; she screams out hoping for rescue as she loses her footing, making a final dive for the bank. She is left clinging to a branch that clearly won't be able to hold her long. A strong arm enters the frame and grabs her wrist, pulling her to safety. The scene ends revealing the rescuer to be Sebastian Lukas, the town sheriff, asking her *"What the hell were you doing down there?"*

#### Title Card

### Wednesday, Sep 1, Present day, approx 2:00 PM

A group of teens is milling about complaining while the sheriff finishes up putting a new chain and lock on the main gate to the Old Quarry. Some of the teens were already there swimming: Annalise, Chris, Hana, a few others; some were there skating: Everette, Trish (who was just watching); and some were fucking around in the woods and came to see what the big deal was: Graye.

Sebastian announces that Brookhaven National Lab has purchased the Old Quarry and intends to prosecute trespassers. The teens declare that this is bullshit, and he should eat socks, but it doesn't seem to bother him.

Trish, Graye, and Everette discuss how much bullshit it is that they would just close such a popular hangout spot without any warning. Trish points out people get hurt there all the time, Graye and Everette point out that that is most of the appeal. Graye and Everette flip off the sheriff and leave, Graye to go back into the woods and Trish/Everette decide to go to the library to skate more. Several large white work vans enter the gate and the sheriff hands over the key after letting them in.

Meanwhile, Chris, Hana, and Annalise have a similar discussion about how dumb it is to just get kicked out. Chris suggests ice cream so they head to *Oh Deer! Cafe* and Hana splits off "to go grab something real quick". On the walk back Chris confesses to Annalise that he and Hana have been dating kind of all summer, and it's going really well. Annalise is hurt they didn't tell her, but Chris admits he just didn't want to make it weird (despite turning it into a whole thing and making it weird now). He doesn't like talking about relationship stuff and was the one to ask Hana to keep it a secret, but she has finally pushed him to tell Annalise because she wants to be able to talk to her about it too. Annalise agrees to sit next to Chris in homeroom against her better judgment.

They get to the cafe and Melody serves their ice cream (Two scoops of cotton candy for Annalise, one strawberry one cookie dough in a waffle cone for Chris, and one scoop of vanilla in a bowl for Hana). Zelda, Mars, and a few others are also there but Annalise and Chris get a table of their own. They debate the wisdom of getting Hana ice cream early or if it will melt. Hana arrives and Annalise tries to act so normal like so so so normal that Hana isn't even sure if Chris told her they were dating. They get it cleared up, and it's awkward again, but the vibe is recovered by debating vanilla vs cotton candy as an ice cream flavor. The trio decide to have a sleepover movie night at Chris' place since his parents are out (unspecified reason).

While the ice cream scene was happening, Everette and Trish walk back towards town. Everette can't let go of needing to go back for answers, and Trish has girl troubles on the mind. She likes Amy who is so sweet and wonderful but literally can't shake the massive crush she has on Aliya and feels bad about it. Everette points out Aliya doesn't like her or even pretend to, but Trish doesn't think that matters. Everette is punctuating statements with sick tricks that he lands like 2/3 of the time. Trish is going to Aliya's back to school party and wants to 'just get Aliya out of my system'. Everette invites her to come back and break into the quarry later tonight and she agrees.

While the ice cream and girl trouble scenes were going on, Graye circles back around to the quarry and enters through a hole in the fence. The majority of it is empty, but the work vans are parked outside the old foreman's trailer along with a viper green Dodge Charger with black stripes up the middle. The contractors from the vans are inspecting the trailer and talking about what bad shape its in. A tall, tan guy with dark slicked back hair, wearing a muscle tee exits the douchebag car. He seems to be in charge. The group is concerned about working quickly but agrees the trailer is in such rough condition they'll have to bring in a new one. The muscle tee guy says he'll talk to the director and the contractors give the impression the director will be mad about any delay or extra cost. The muscle tee guy gets back in his car and drives away blaring Metallica.

### Later that night, approx 10:00 PM 
Kat makes the players each choose a number (1, 2, 3) and rolls a die to decide who will find the body to try and figure out how to work it into the story. The players are terrified.

Annalise, Chris and Hana are having a cute sleepover with popcorn and slasher movies under a blanket fort in the living room. Annalise rolls to use her death sense but can only determine "No one has died in the past going to a sleepover with Chris and Hana". Once again, the players are very stressed.

Cut to Everette and Trish. Everette is wearing dark green cargo shorts, a blink 182 shirt and a beanie. Trish is wearing black jeans, a black turtleneck, gloves, black sneakers, a beanie, and has painted her face a smokey dark gray. She spent all afternoon researching how to do a Breaking and Entering crime, and her search history is probably wildly incriminating. The main gate is locked and deserted so the pair sneak in across a fallen tree that had pushed down a section of fence. Everette checks no one is around and gets a running start to skateboard grind the length of the tree. They head to the foreman's trailer which is now locked and Trish breaks like 6 bobby pins off in the lock trying to emulate a video she watched. Everette takes over and smashes the lock off with a rock. As Everette enters the trailer we did a super dramatic cut to Graye.

Graye snuck in the back fence and had already checked the main swimming hole and the skate park (both empty) when they hear Everette smash the lock. They try to use wolf senses but are mostly distracted by some jerky on the ground. Graye leaves the shadows and scares Trish really, really bad, she screams and Everette runs out of the trailer skateboard at the ready to clobber someone. All three of them investigate the trailer, but it seems to be tool storage for now. Everette sees a flash of light in the trees off towards the other swimming hole and the trio goes running, very careful to not leave Trish alone because they think it will keep her from dying.

They arrive at the swimming hole in time to see someone's tail lights as they drive away. None of the characters are car people so no additional info is gleaned. Graye is overwhelmed by the scent of someone/something that was described as 'smells like they are big dog, and you are little dog, no matter how tough you feel as a wolf'. Everette is the first to point a flashlight in the water revealing a body floating face down surrounded by quite a bit of blood in the water. Her purple hair identifies the body as Melody Evermore. Trish goes to puke in a bush and no one does anything until Everette tries to wade in to get her. The other two stop him, not wanting to leave evidence, and they talk through not calling the cops vs calling them. Trish makes the anonymous call and hangs up quickly. The group notice blood on the shore like Melody was attacked there before falling into the water.

## Follow-up
### Thursday, Sep 2, 2004
**Official Statement**
News travels fast in Oak Vale, bad news even faster. It’s not that the sheriff was particularly slow with his announcement but by the time he took the podium for a press conference at 9:03 AM sharp the whole town had already heard the news.

Melody Evermore had been in a terrible accident. 

The rumors were thick. That she was dead, that she was alive, that she was in the ICU. That it was a wild animal, or her older boyfriend, or an OD, or the thieves that broke in to steal the tools BNL had left did it and called the cops after. That it was the most blood the EMTs had ever seen. That the lab had killed her for getting too close to an extraterrestrial and bought the quarry to cover it up.

You know, just the normal types of small town gossip. 

Sebastian Lukas tried to keep his statement concise. 

"Last night, our town experienced a tragedy. I know how the rumor mill works overtime here so I’d like to clear some things up."

"Melody Evermore was seriously injured in the Old Quarry last night. She is receiving care at Roanoke General and is expected to make a full recovery."

"We are also investigating a break in at the quarry, and it currently seems the thieves were the ones who alerted emergency services to Ms Evermore’s condition. It is only by the grace of God and their selfless action that we did not lose one of our own."

"We suspect it was a wild animal attack based on available evidence as well as an increase in aggressive coyote behavior across the county." 

"We do not believe this is related to the attacks at Camp Daylight earlier this week or that there was any foul play involved." 

"If you have information about either incident please contact the sheriff's office." 

"Stay safe and avoid traveling alone after dark until we can track down the animals responsible. Thank you and I will not be answering any questions at this time."

**Back to the MCs**
Graye calls the hospital and confirms Melody is a patient and has visiting hours which implies she is not in critical condition despite how she looked in the quarry. Trish is freaking out and would've gotten an early start texting Everette repeatedly before both of them showed up at Graye's house for more investigating. The best lead they get is Trish asking Amanda and Missy (Melody's two closest friends) for details which reveals three things: 
- Melody doesn’t remember much of the day leading up to the accident, including why she would’ve been in the quarry in the middle of the night.
- She is more than a bit shaken up but probably won’t have to even spend the weekend in the hospital.
- The official theory is that she was attacked by a coyote, fell in the water and hit her head, then barely made it back to shore before going unconscious which is where and how the sheriff found her.
