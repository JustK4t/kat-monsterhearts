A gray sedan speeds past the camera startling some wildlife and around 30 seconds later several unmarked white vans follow. The gray sedan tips one tire in the gravel besides the road, almost rights itself, but overcorrects and slams into a guardrail before rolling easily 100ft (30m) down the road. It doesn't stop rolling until colliding with a tree, and we watch as the passenger is ejected, launched farther down the cliffside.
